# Observing Log: Messier, Caldwell and Herschel 400

An observing log for the Astronomical League Messier, Binocular Messier, Caldwell and Herschel 400 programs. Every entry is checked against the program's required fields (time, latitude and longitude, seeing, transparency, aperture, power, description), the unseen objects are sorted by how high they are tonight, and it prints the log your society officer reviews. Red light mode, works offline once loaded.

Use it: https://samsbench.com/observing-log/

Free for Messier and Binocular Messier. No sign-up. Your log stays in your browser; back it up to a file.
Not made by or affiliated with the Astronomical League.
